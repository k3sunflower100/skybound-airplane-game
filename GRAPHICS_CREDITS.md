# Graphics credits

- Earth imagery: NASA/Goddard Space Flight Center Scientific Visualization Studio, *Blue Marble: A Seamless Image Mosaic of the Earth* (MODIS satellite observations). Data courtesy of Reto Stockli (NASA/GSFC) and NASA Earth Observatory. https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/2915
- Post-processing: Three.js r128 example passes (EffectComposer, RenderPass, UnrealBloomPass, BokehPass), distributed under the Three.js MIT license. https://github.com/mrdoob/three.js/blob/r128/LICENSE
- Aircraft: original procedural game geometry inspired by modern fighter silhouettes. The five selector entries are playable visual profiles, not manufacturer-supplied or certified 3D models.
