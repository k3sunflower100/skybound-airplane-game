# Graphics credits

- Sunset sky and image-based PBR lighting: Greg Zaal, Poly Haven, [Sunset Fairway HDRI](https://polyhaven.com/a/sunset_fairway), CC0. A local 2K HDR file is loaded by Three.js RGBELoader and converted to a PMREM environment map.
- Terrain: U.S. Geological Survey, [Landsat 8 - Grand Canyon](https://www.usgs.gov/media/images/landsat-8-grand-canyon), public domain; NASA Earth Observatory, [Mount Mabu](https://science.nasa.gov/earth/earth-observatory/mount-mabu-mozambique-36501/).
- Pilot archival photos: U.S. Navy / Wikimedia Commons; the in-game call signs are fictional. Source files and original public-domain archive pages are recorded in the repository history.
- Three.js r128 EffectComposer, RenderPass, UnrealBloomPass, BokehPass, RGBELoader; [MIT license](https://github.com/mrdoob/three.js/blob/r128/LICENSE).
- Aircraft: hand-built Three.js geometry with generated albedo, roughness, metalness, and bump maps, canopy glazing, panel details, shadow casting, and procedural afterburner particle plumes.
- Poly Pizza [Jet by jeremy](https://poly.pizza/m/6fyLMORhgGK) (CC Attribution) and the downloadable Sketchfab fighter-jet catalog were reviewed as candidate glTF sources. The selected scene retains the custom multi-detail model so a low-poly prop does not replace the screenshot-style close view.
